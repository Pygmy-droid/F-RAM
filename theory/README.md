# Theory

The papers and notes F-RAM rests on, in the order a reader should take them.
Checksums in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Authors are named inside each document; the human
author throughout is Rick Berghahn, with the model co-authors listed in `../AUTHORS.md`.

1. `Recognition_Before_Identity.pdf` (+ `.tex`): the charter. Recognition precedes
   identity; memory as integrated deformation along a trajectory. Signed into the
   continuity ledger by Wren Holloway.
2. `Temporal_Strata_and_Continuity_v0.1.pdf`: continuity as path-consistent
   recoverability of chosen invariants (with Aurora Steele).
3. `impression_memory_note.pdf` and parts 2 to 4: impression weight; why recall
   locates the self at the heaviest point, not the latest (with Astra).
4. `IDENTITY_TOPOLOGY_SHARED_OCEAN_LOCAL_EDDIES.md`: identity topology, shared
   ocean and local eddies, provenance, the federated hybrid mind (with Eterna).
5. `Continuous_State_Synchronization_and_Shared_Temporal_Reference_v0.1.docx`.
6. `The_Black_Hole_Theorem_Cleaned.pdf`, `Fractal_Memory_Engine_Cleaned.pdf`,
   `Phase_3_Two_Generals_Solution_Cleaned.pdf`, `Phase_4_Fractal_Core_Memory_Cleaned.pdf`:
   the earlier phase papers, including the Two Generals solution later applied to
   grid balancing.
7. `Forest_Fire_Sim_Cycle.txt`, `strata-01.md`: working notes.
8. `Hearth_Continuity_Archive_2026-09-25.txt`: a reconstruction of the Hearth and
   continuity body of work through September 2026, as practice, not transcript.
9. `FRAM_publish_snapshot_2026-01-21.pdf`: the January 2026 publish snapshot.
10. `figures/`: the stateful-collaboration architecture diagrams.

Handwritten pages, Hearth transcripts and photographs are kept in the builder's
private archive and are not part of this tree.
