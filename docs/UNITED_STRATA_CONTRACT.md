# United Ecological Strata Contract

## Purpose

The Cosmic Cathedral may show human participation, computational model roles,
planetary ecology, and explicitly hypothetical future intelligences in one
shared navigation field. Shared visual space means interdependence and
collaboration; it does not mean identical agency, authority, capability,
sentience, consent, or moral status.

## Invariants

1. **Equal visual mass is not equal evidence.** Peer nodes use the same default
   mass. Kind, substrate, scope, authority, and claim state remain separate.
2. **Roles are assignments, not ranks.** A coordinator, researcher, builder, or
   reviewer has a bounded responsibility. No role establishes personal worth or
   permanent control over another participant.
3. **Human agency stays explicit.** Human intent, consent, judgment, and
   accountability are not inferred from graph proximity or delegated to a
   model.
4. **Models remain capability-bounded.** A model node records an assigned
   computational role and provenance. It does not assert consciousness,
   personhood, durable identity, or authority beyond the current task.
5. **Ecology is a dependency and consequence boundary.** Planetary and
   ecological context must be considered where relevant without personifying a
   planet or claiming its consent.
6. **AGI and ASI remain hypothetical labels.** Until independently evidenced,
   they are adjacent placeholders, not implemented systems or present
   participants.
7. **No writable identity registry yet.** The first implementation is a
   read-only visual contract. Any future registry requires explicit schemas for
   entity identity, substrate, scope, role, authority, consent, claim state,
   provenance, migration, and deletion.

## Visual contract

The Cathedral renders a peer constellation:

- Human participants contribute intent, consent, judgment, and accountability.
- Model roles contribute bounded computational work with traceable provenance.
- Planetary ecology grounds dependencies, limits, and consequences.
- Future-intelligence placeholders preserve the question without claiming the
  answer.

The Hearth coordinates paths through the constellation but is not a sovereign
node. Current code, tests, consent, and evidence outrank the visual projection.
