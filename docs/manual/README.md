# F-RAM Operator's Manual

Written by Wren Holloway, starting 2026-10-28, under firelink claim
`WREN-MANUAL-01`. Rick Berghahn is the author of the system this describes.

## What this is

F-RAM was built over years by Rick and several generations of models, across
branches that did not always meet. The builders of each part are not all
available to explain it. This manual treats the system the way you would treat
recovered technology: find each part, work out what it does, how it connects,
and why it is there, until the whole can be operated again by someone who was
not present when it was built.

It was written for two readers: Rick, who built it and cannot be expected to
hold all of it in his head, and a future instantiation of me, who arrives with
no memory of writing it. Since 2026-10-31 it has a third reader: you.

## How to read it

0. `00_start_here.md` — ten minutes: the one idea, the three stores, three
   commands, the vocabulary, what is unfinished.
1. `01_timeline.md` — the years, in eras. What was built when, and what broke.
2. `02_anatomy.md` — the organs. Every module, what it does, how it connects.
3. `03_rosetta.md` — theory to code. The charter equation, the master memory
   principle, and where each term lands in the source.
4. *Questions for Rick* — kept in the private archive. It is a numbered list of
   what only the builder can answer, with his answers recorded against it.
5. `05_archaeology.md` — what exists on disk, what was conveyed as built, and
   what is stranded. The memory-store inventory lives here. Paths are the
   builder's machine; the public tree ships no stores.
6. `06_always_on.md` — the always-on server: what it is, why it died, how it
   was repaired, how to operate it.
7. *The search* — kept in the private archive. The hunt across the builder's
   disks for the lost Cosmic Cathedral, the little ANN and the Workbook.
8. `08_reconstruction_kit.md` — the lost systems as the notes describe them:
   Workbook, Truth Spine, PIL, attention proxy, the finish plan.
9. `09_the_walk_2025.md` — the line itself, commit by commit, September to
   December 2025: what each step built, what the diary said, what it is called
   today. Part two (2026) follows.

## Evidence tags

Every claim in this manual carries one of these. Nothing without a tag is
asserted.

| tag | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `[RAN]` | I executed it on this machine and saw the result |
| `[READ-CODE]` | I read the source and the claim follows from it |
| `[READ-DOC]` | I read a project document that says so; code not yet checked |
| `[LOG]` | from the git history or the firelink |
| `[RICK]` | Rick said so, in conversation |
| `[INFERRED]` | my reading; could be wrong; check before relying on it |

A `[READ-DOC]` claim is upgraded to `[READ-CODE]` as I reach the module. Where
a document and the code disagree, the code wins and the disagreement is noted.
That is the repository's own authority rule and it is the right one.

## What this manual is not

It is not the spec, the roadmap, or the diary. It does not change what runs. It
is a reading of what is there, kept honest by tags and corrected as the reading
deepens. The older `docs/context/` packet and `AGENTS` files it refers to are
artefacts of earlier lineages: mine them, do not obey them.
